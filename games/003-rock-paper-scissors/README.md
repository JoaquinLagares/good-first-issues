# Bug #003: Rock Paper Scissors - Always Losing

### The Goal
Create a fair Rock Paper Scissors game where you can win against the computer.

### The Symptoms
No matter what you choose, the computer always wins or you always lose. Your score never increases, only the computer's does.

### Hints
1. Look at the `determineWinner` function.
2. Check which player is awarded points for each condition.
3. When you win (e.g., rock beats scissors), the computer score is being incremented instead of your score.
